## Ratchet ruling — FRESH, as commissioned

**RULED: the ratchet family (`liminal22`, `sleepreap`) does NOT enter
this cut. It remains staging line 3, its scripts untouched, its five
checks unabsorbed — and this cut carries one affirmative duty toward
it, stated below.** Argued from the design, not inherited:

1. **The five checks are verdict-side prior art, and this cut is the
   assembly layer.** Vanished-red-is-red, suite-count-drift, the
   absent/malformed stderr distinction, named-hole verdicts, named
   exit codes — every one is a property of gate-entry MEASUREMENT
   (what a gate run observes and how it names what it saw). Their
   lift target is the gate-leg command surface (cut-1's family,
   extended), not recipes. Amendment 1 makes recipes structurally
   unreachable from gate-leg context — absorbing gate-verdict
   machinery into the one layer gate legs cannot reach would be a
   category error at the grammar level, not just a scheduling choice.
2. **The Q4 pricing law forbids compounding behaviour-shaped
   cutovers.** "A cutover of a data-shaped set is recoverable, a
   cutover of a behaviour-shaped set is not" (Q4 §5). This cut is
   already the behaviour-shaped one of the line — assembly acts,
   projection, receipts. Adding the ratchet family's five behaviours
   would stack the line's two hardest cutovers in one flight, on the
   cut that also carries the third-slot schedule weight.
3. **The checks lift onto a LANDED surface, and cut 1 is mid-flight.**
   Amendment 2's own words: the checks "exist as requirements in the
   new surface" before any retirement. Requirements written today
   would pin to a surface whose landed bytes this brief has not seen
   (the conformance read is this flight's FIRST act precisely because
   the landing may diverge) — a pin asserting today's mechanism,
   fighting tomorrow's landing.
4. **The one-vocabulary construction removes the rework argument.**
   The only case FOR early absorption is that recipe surfaces built
   without ratchet in view would need rework when it lands. The §6
   close forecloses this: gate verdicts, control replies, and builder
   refusals share one closed-cause record discipline, so a recipe
   step that consumes verdict records consumes the vocabulary, not
   any family's private shape. The affirmative duty below makes that
   mechanical rather than assumed.

**The affirmative duty (this cut's, binding, folded into R5):** the
specimen-firing compare surface is typed over the ONE vocabulary's
record shape — the invariant, never an enumeration of diff-census
verdicts. Proven by fixture: at least one ratchet-SHAPED record (a
named-hole verdict; an absent-vs-malformed pair as two distinct
